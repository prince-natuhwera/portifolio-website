Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about my services

What areas do you serve?

I work with clients globally, providing remote GIS consulting, training, and Power BI dashboard development services. For training and workshops, I can travel to your location if needed.

What is your typical project process?

My project process typically involves an initial consultation, needs assessment, proposal development, project execution, and follow-up support. I maintain open communication throughout to ensure your needs are met.

What GIS software and tools do you work with?

I have expertise in a wide range of GIS software including ArcGIS (Desktop, Pro, Online), QGIS, Power BI for spatial data visualization, Google Earth Engine, and various programming tools like Python for geospatial analysis.

How do you price your services?

Pricing depends on the scope, complexity, and duration of your project. I offer both hourly rates and project-based pricing. Contact me for a customized quote based on your specific needs.
