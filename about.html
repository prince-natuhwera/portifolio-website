About Me

Get to know my background, expertise, and approach

Who I Am

I am a multidisciplinary GIS, Remote Sensing, and Land Surveying professional with over 5 years of hands-on experience in geospatial data analysis, GNSS field operations, and project coordination across sectors including land adjudication, mineral exploration, and environmental monitoring.

With a background in Land Surveying and Geomatics from Makerere University, I've worked with diverse clients ranging from government agencies to international organizations, helping them implement effective geospatial solutions that drive growth and efficiency.

My mission is to bridge the gap between complex spatial data and actionable insights, empowering organizations to make informed, data-driven decisions through innovative GIS solutions, interactive visualizations, and comprehensive training.

My Expertise

Specialized skills and knowledge areas

Geospatial Analysis

Advanced spatial analysis, geodatabase design and management, and visualization techniques to extract meaningful patterns from geographical data.

Remote Sensing

Processing and interpreting satellite imagery for environmental monitoring, water quality assessment, and resource management using tools like SNAP and Sentinel-3.

Land Surveying

GNSS field operations, topographic surveys, cadastral surveying, and laser scanning for accurate spatial data collection and land documentation.

Dashboard Development

Creating interactive data dashboards using Power BI for project monitoring, grievance tracking, and visualization of complex spatial data.

Mobile Data Collection

Design and deployment of mobile data collection systems using QField Cloud, ArcGIS Survey123, ODK, and KoboCollect for efficient field operations.

GIS Training

Customized training programs and workshops for individuals and organizations on various GIS platforms, tools, and methodologies.
